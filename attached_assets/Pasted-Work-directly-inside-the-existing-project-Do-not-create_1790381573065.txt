8. FIX THE KNOWN SEGMENT PROBLEMS

Early transition

If Arabic audio ends before the video segment ends, do NOT immediately move to the next segment.

Transition must follow the actual video timeline and valid synchronization data.

Seek

If the user seeks to "07:13", do not snap back to "07:00".

Keep the exact requested position, find the correct segment, calculate the internal offset, and synchronize the Arabic audio to that exact position.

videoRate

Synchronization must correctly account for the real video playback rate.

Never assume "videoRate = 1.0".

Use:

- current video time,
- current audio time,
- segment start,
- segment end,
- audio duration,
- audio offset,
- manual offset,
- video rate,
- speech timing.

Prefetch race conditions

If the user seeks while the next segment is processing, obsolete background results must not overwrite the new playback state.

Use stable IDs/versioning to invalidate stale work.
